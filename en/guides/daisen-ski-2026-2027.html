Skiing in Daisen

Daisen Ski Area, 2026-27 Season
Opening Dates and Lift Ticket Prices

Information as of November 11, 2026

The ski area on Mt. Daisen has a new name this season: Daisen Mountain Resort (formerly Daisen White Resort). Here is a summary of the 2026-27 season schedule and lift ticket and season pass prices, based on the resort's official website, which is currently available in Japanese only.

1. Season Schedule

  • Pass holders receive extra benefits: three half-price 1-day ticket coupons for an accompanying adult, five ¥500 coupons for the on-site restaurants, discounted parking tickets, 50% off 1-day tickets at partner ski areas through the Snow Birds Club, and discounts at hot springs and restaurants (After Ski Premium).

  • Passes are ordered through the resort's Japanese-language form or by mail, and paid by bank transfer within Japan. The discount does not apply if payment arrives after November 27. Details are on the season pass page (Japanese).

4. Getting There and Winter Driving

  • The ski area is at 96 Daisen, Daisen-cho, Saihaku-gun, Tottori, in the same Daisen village as Scène (115-7 Daisen).
  • By bus, the nearest stop for both the ski area and Scène is "Daisenji." Take the Nihon Kotsu bus bound for Daisenji from JR Yonago Station (about 50 minutes).
  • Winter parking costs ¥1,000 per car, with about 1,500 spaces just below the slopes. The lots can fill up on busy days.
  • Roads freeze from December to March, and winter tires or snow chains are required. If you rent a car, make sure it comes with winter tires.

The dates and prices in this article are taken from the Daisen Mountain Resort official website (Japanese) as of November 11, 2026. They may change, so please check the official website for the latest information.
